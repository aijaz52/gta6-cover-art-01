FROM PHOTO TO COVER

Three steps to your
next alter ego.

  1. Bring a photo. Drop in a JPG, PNG or WebP, or start with our original illustrated scene.
  2. Make it yours. Add a caption, choose a panel arrangement and tune the color story. Crop controls adjust your photo across the panels.
  3. Take it with you. Export a clean PNG at 1200 × 1500 or 2400 × 3000. Everything happens on your device.

Tip: press Ctrl/⌘ + Enter to export. After your first visit, the local web app can reopen offline. Your photo stays in memory and is cleared when you reload.
